## What we hope for

We carry the gospel to those who come to Boston as sojourners, make them
disciples of Jesus, and send them into the world.

Boston is a city people pass through. They arrive to study, to work, to stay a
while. However long that stay turns out to be, we hope it becomes a time of
meeting God closely.

## What we hold to

### Scripture, daily

Worship does not end on Sunday. Through the week we practise holding to the
word, each of us where we are.

### One congregation, every age

Children and adults worship in the same room. Rather than separating the
generations, we keep a place where each learns the faith by watching the other.

### Faith shared in Korean

Services are held in Korean, and we keep a place where the mother tongue can
carry prayer and confession freely. In an unfamiliar country, that is no small
comfort.
